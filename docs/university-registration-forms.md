# University Registration Forms

The existing /register design is preserved and the university path now uses three steps.

## Step 1: Account Manager

Full name, job title, official account email, phone, password, and password confirmation.

## Step 2: Institution

English and Arabic names, description, type, location, country, city, address, website, and contact data.

## Step 3: Identity And Accreditation

Official university email, official contact, official phone, email domain, logo URL, license/accreditation number, accreditation document URL, notes, and terms acceptance.

Validation covers email, password strength, matching password, required institution fields, URL format, and terms. Development autofill fills fields without submitting.

Compatibility: existing universityName, description, location, website, phone, officialContact, and logo payloads remain accepted.
