# University Reports

Page: /university/reports.

Supported sources: university summary, students, readiness, employment, skill gaps, college comparison, department comparison, curriculum alignment, curriculum recommendations, and audit history.

Formats:

- CSV: UTF-8 with BOM.
- XLSX: a real Office workbook generated by ExcelJS.
- PDF: a real PDF document generated by PDFKit.
- JSON: preview contract for the page.

- docs/generated-reports/university-summary.xlsx: 10,010 bytes, ZIP/Office PK, 16 entries.
